A Message from Raj Kumar

Every year, our industry produces countless trend reports—many focusing on destinations or experience types generating momentary interest. While these offer valuable perspectives, they often miss what matters most to those of us running travel businesses: the operational reality of selling, planning, and delivering multi-day experiences.

That's why we've prepared this report. Our goal is to surface the business side of the story—what it actually looks like to operate in today's market, grounded in real data rather than speculation.

What makes this perspective possible is our unique vantage point. At ORN Trip Factory, we work with businesses across almost every country in the world. Looking back at last year's activity, we observed more than 350,000 multi-day trips and 800,000 bookings. But booking data alone doesn't tell the whole story.

This report also draws from our Annual Travel Trends Survey, in which operators, DMCs, suppliers, and advisors share what they're seeing inside their own businesses. Together, these two perspectives—observed behavior and reported experience—allow us to move beyond isolated data points and understand the broader forces shaping our industry.

Rather than offering predictions of what might happen next, this report shows what is already happening, so businesses can see beyond their own booking dashboards and understand how their experience fits into the larger landscape.

When we stepped back and looked at the data as a whole, a clear picture emerged: trips cost more, expectations are higher, bookings increasingly involve groups rather than individuals, and businesses are navigating growing operational complexity across every stage of the journey.

Strategic Implications

What This Means for Travel Businesses

1 Make Customization Easy

Customization is now a baseline expectation. Allow travelers to select and pay for add-ons at booking. Use AI-powered itinerary builders to personalize faster without rebuilding from scratch.

2 Use Payment Plans

With travelers committing to higher-priced trips by paying over time, payment plans are no longer optional. Ensure your booking system supports installments by default.

3 Strengthen Partner Networks

More experience-driven itineraries require partners outside traditional networks. Find, vet, and work with a wider range of local partners to add new elements without increasing delivery risk.

4 Build AI Discovery Foundation

Start building visibility now. AI search and social platforms are already shaping how travelers discover trips. Focus on clear, well-structured trip pages that surface in traditional and AI search engines.

5 Start Using AI Operations

Integrate AI into everyday workflows starting with simple applications like research and drafting. This reduces manual workload and builds familiarity as AI-supported practices become standard.

6 Ensure Frictionless Booking

As more travelers are funneled from discovery channels to your website, the booking experience becomes critical. A smooth, integrated checkout helps convert interest into confirmed bookings.
